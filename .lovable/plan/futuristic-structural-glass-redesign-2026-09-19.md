# Futuristic Structural-Glass Redesign

## Goal
Apply the selected geometric structural-glass direction across the full STRUCTRA website, making every page feel like part of the monitored product system while preserving all existing content, navigation, interactions, and no-pricing constraint.

## Visual System
- Lock the palette to Midnight Signal: deep ink surfaces, cyan signal light, and restrained violet depth.
- Replace the current heading treatment with Space Grotesk and use DM Sans for body copy; retain JetBrains Mono for telemetry labels.
- Introduce a reusable structural-glass language: transparent technical panels, clipped corners, exposed frame edges, coordinate marks, and connected signal lines.
- Strengthen the monitored-building background with dimensional floor plates, sensor nodes, scanning light, depth falloff, and crisp high-resolution linework.

## Pages and Components
- Rework the shared header and footer into precise instrument rails so the visual direction carries across every page.
- Upgrade reusable sections, cards, metrics, forms, calls to action, portraits, and partner panels without changing their content.
- Refine the Home hero to match the selected composition: centered high-impact statement, structural framing, product telemetry, and quiet cinematic motion.
- Apply the same visual grammar to Product, Proof, Board, Group, Contact, and Privacy.

## Technical Details
- Define all colors, shadows, geometry, and animation through semantic tokens and reusable classes in the global stylesheet.
- Keep interactive controls accessible and unobstructed; preserve reduced-motion behavior.
- Verify the finished site on desktop and mobile, including every content page and existing links.
