# Structra Signal Hub

You are a elite UI/UX designer and full-stack web developer specializing in high-converting B2B industrial-tech and enterprise SaaS websites.



Objective: Build a high-end, modern, conversion-focused website from scratch for STRUCTRA (note: official spelling is STRUCTRA, getstructra.com).

1. Brand Identity & Visual Vibe

Aesthetic: High-tech, institutional, precise, dark-mode primary theme with minimalist accents (similar to Linear.app, Vercel, or Stripe for industrial hardware).

Color Palette: Deep matte dark grey/black background (#0A0A0C), crisp stark white typography, and vibrant precision signal accents (electric amber or neon blue) to signify active continuous monitoring signals.

Typography: Clean, modern sans-serif fonts (e.g., Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or SF Pro).

2. Key Messaging & Copy Baseline

Hero Headline: Continuous Signal. Engineering Intelligence.



Value Proposition: Always-on structural intelligence for commercial properties, prefab assets, and infrastructure. Bridging the invisible gaps between periodic engineering visits.

Primary CTAs: "Request a Pilot" and "Explore Pricing".

3. Page Structure & Section Breakdown

Header Navigation:

Logo: STRUCTRA

Nav Items: Product, Proof, Pricing, Team, Contact

CTA Button: Book a 90-Day Pilot

Section 1: Hero Section

Bold headline + subtitle summarizing continuous structural health monitoring (SHM).

High-tech visual mockups: Structural nodes emitting live telemetry, anomaly detection graphs, and real-time alert badges.

Trust Bar: "Incorporated in Dubai South Business Hub Free Zone" | "Supported by Technostream Network".

Section 2: The Problem vs. The Structra Solution

Interactive comparison highlighting Periodic Inspections (blind snapshots, reactive) vs. Structra Continuous Signal (always-on, proactive baseline tracking).

Section 3: How It Works (The 3-Step Stack)



01 / Capture (Structra Node): Wireless sensing for fast installation and continuous vibration capture.



02 / Learn (Structra Cloud): Baseline detection and live frequency deviation flagging.



03 / Flag (Structra Dashboard): Unified interface for asset alerts, baseline trends, and instant anomaly investigation.

Section 4: Technical Proof & Validation

Highlight laboratory performance: Dual MPU-6050 accelerometers on physical truss models, <1s response time, 100% anomaly detection rate.

Award Badge: 3rd Place out of 50+ Teams at the AUS Physics Competition.

Section 5: Transparent Pricing Architecture

Interactive pricing calculator or clear tier cards:



Structra Node: $200 per node (Volume discounts: $185 at 10+, $165 at 50+).



Site Activation: $750+ (Gateway hardware, setup, commissioning).



Monitoring SaaS: $55/mo (1–4 nodes), $95/mo (5–8 nodes), $165/mo (9–16 nodes).

Highlight: Pilot Properties start at AED 2,500.

Section 6: Competitive Position

Modern comparison chart showing Structra’s low entry barrier (~AED 2,500 pilot) versus legacy BAS/SHM systems ($26K–$287K+).

Section 7: Team & Leadership
